We are building a new lab in Hangzhou and would love to hear from future colleagues and collaborators.

Join the lab

We are recruiting the founding members of the lab. If you are excited to discover new biology and develop new technologies, at the bench and with AI, we would love to hear from you.

  • Postdoctoral fellows / Research staff

    Molecular biology, biochemistry, structural biology or gene therapy.

  • PhD students

    Students who plan to apply to the PhD program at Zhejiang University School of Medicine are warmly welcome to get in touch.

Who we look for

Curious, rigorous people who enjoy hard problems and working in a team. We value diverse backgrounds: biochemistry, structural biology, microbiology and genome engineering all fit here.

What you will learn

Hands-on training across the full arc of our work: discovering systems by mining sequence and structure data, investigating their function with biochemical and cell-based assays, solving their structures, and engineering them into tools, with close mentorship along the way.

Where you will be

Our lab will be at Zijingang (紫金港), Zhejiang University’s main campus: lakes, open lawns and room to think. Around you, a thriving academic community across medicine, biology, engineering and AI, with excellent core facilities.

To apply, email peiyu.xu@hotmail.com with your CV and a short note about your interests.
